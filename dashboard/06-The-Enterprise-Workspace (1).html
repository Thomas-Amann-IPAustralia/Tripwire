Team controls

Trust is also about who can do what.

  • Review permissions: who can acknowledge an assessment and record a decision.
  • Configuration permissions: who can change thresholds or monitored sources.
  • History: what was changed, by whom and when.
These are design requirements, not assertions about the existing authentication or audit implementation.
Lookbook / The Considered Workspace / Version 2

Design system

Warmth and a touch of whimsy, without sacrificing the habits that make software credible.

Character study
The case for this direction

A trusted workspace with a pulse.

Tripwire needs the familiar structure of a serious business tool, but it does not need to look as though it was assembled from grey boxes. A warm paper palette, editorial headings and small signal-inspired details make it feel considered and human. The evidence views, review states and decisions remain admirably ordinary.

Recognisable at first glanceKeep the stable sidebar, conventional tables, search, filters, traceable evidence and review drawers.
Personality in the marginsUse little visual signatures, warm surfaces and inviting typography in navigation, headers and empty states.
Restraint where it mattersNever decorate a warning, change a status colour for fun or let illustration compete with source evidence.
