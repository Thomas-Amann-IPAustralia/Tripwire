Semantic filtering
Progressively more precise — and more expensive — matching narrows the field to the pages a change truly affects.

Stage 4relevance scoring
Relevance Scoring
Which IPFR pages could this change touch? Two signals fused by weighted RRF before any heavy work.
BM25 — YAKE keyphrases
Bi-encoder — BGE cosine
RRF — semantic 2× keyword (k=60)
Top-N + threshold + fast-pass stop a major change being capped at 5 pages.
Stage 5bi-encoder
Bi-Encoder Matching
A coarse chunk-level pass. The change is chunked, BGE-encoded, and cosine-compared to every precomputed IPFR chunk.
Candidate if
any single chunk ≥ 0.75
or ≥ 3 chunks from a page ≥ 0.45
Cheap dot products over embeddings already built during ingestion.
Stage 6cross-encoder
Cross-Encoder Refine
Precise reranking of survivors with a gte-reranker (8,192-token window), fusing semantic, lexical & graph signals.
Rerank — CE + lexical + graph
Propagate — along edges
  (decay 0.45/hop, ≤ 3 hops)
Neighbours only boost, never lower; pages ≥ 0.60 proceed to aggregation.
