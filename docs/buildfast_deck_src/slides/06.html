For over a century, IP Australia has administered intellectual property rights — and largely left their protection up to the people who hold them.
IP First Response is a platform built to demystify the complexity of protecting Australian IP. By surfacing alternatives to court, we make the ecosystem more equitable — raising the real value of an Australian IP right.
