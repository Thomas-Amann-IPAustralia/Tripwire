Additional takeaway
Building fast ≠ learning fast
It's easy to build things faster than you can learn about them — leaving you at the mercy of the LLM's judgement.*
* not necessarily a terrible thing.
%%ICON compass var(--blue) 30%%
Key suggestion
Invest time in understanding the solution you're directing. Read blogs, watch videos, and iterate on a project brief with a smart LLM. The tokens you spend understanding pay for themselves.
