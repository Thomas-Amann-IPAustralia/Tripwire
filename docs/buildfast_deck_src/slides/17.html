Where it broke
Scaling the rulebook surfaced fundamental issues
What we builtnaïve v1
Every rule fired at every sentence — with no idea whether it was reading a heading, a legal disclaimer, a table cell or a link.
%%ICON xc var(--red) 24%%
It runs every rule, everywhere
No sense of which checks are even relevant to the sentence in front of it.
%%ICON xc var(--red) 24%%
It has no context
A quick note and a formal letter are judged by exactly the same rules.
%%ICON xc var(--red) 24%%
Many "rules" are judgement calls
Tone and appropriateness aren't yes/no — automatable, but far more complex, and no longer just code.
%%ICON route var(--green) 26%%
What we needed
Parse the document's structure first · route each element to the rules that actually apply · only then judge.
