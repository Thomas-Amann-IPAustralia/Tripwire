The influenced corpus · ingestion, part 1
Capturing our own site
Runs automatically before the Tripwire check — so the corpus is always current before anything is compared against it.
1
Sitemap harvesting
Read the IPFR sitemap; register every page with its ID, URL, title and snapshot path.
2
Normalisation
trafilatura strips nav, footers and sidebars. Plain text is the single canonical representation.
3
SHA-256 versioning
Hash the normalised text. If the hash matches, the page is skipped — no work, no cost.
4
Section-aware chunking
Split at real heading boundaries, not every 1,400 characters. A chunk should be about one thing.
5
Cron-based maintenance
Every 24 hours, forever, with no human involved. State is committed back to the repo.
