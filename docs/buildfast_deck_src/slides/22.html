What Tripwire does, in three moves
Detect. Connect. Recommend.
01 · Detectthe outside world
%%ICON activity var(--blue) 40%%
Spots real changes
Watches all 156 sources on their own schedules — and ignores cosmetic churn: reformatting, timestamps, menu shuffles.
02 · Connectthe connection
%%ICON share var(--blue) 40%%
Finds affected pages
Searches our 139 pages by exact words and by meaning, then shortlists the few a change could actually touch.
03 · Recommendthe judgement
%%ICON mailcheck var(--blue) 40%%
Recommends an action
Drafts the case for an amendment — or "no change needed", or "uncertain, please look" — and emails the owner.
%%ICON clock var(--ink3) 20%%
Runs unattended every morning. Tripwire recommends — it never edits the website.
