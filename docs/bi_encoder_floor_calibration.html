How we set the bi-encoder floor at 0.63

The backfilled scores let us test a threshold against ground truth before shipping it. The result: a floor at 0.63 drops a quarter of the noise while keeping every real signal — because the two classes visibly separate on this one score.

Why it holds

The floor works because the classes separate on this score. Every real signal scored at least 0.666, so a cut at 0.63 keeps all of them; meanwhile a quarter of the noise scored below 0.63 and is removed before it ever reaches the LLM. The 0.036 gap between the floor and the lowest real signal is the safety margin — deliberately thin, because it rests on just 21 confirmed signals. That is why 0.63 was chosen over the tighter 0.65, and why the margin is re-checked as more signals accrue.
