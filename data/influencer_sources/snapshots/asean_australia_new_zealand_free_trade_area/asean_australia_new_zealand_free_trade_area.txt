The Agreement Establishing the ASEAN–Australia–New Zealand Free Trade Area (AANZFTA) is a comprehensive trade and investment agreement between Australia, New Zealand and ASEAN Member States: Brunei Darussalam, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam.
AANZFTA reduces tariffs and enhances certainty for businesses by providing stable and predictable conditions for services suppliers and investors. It is widely regarded as one of ASEAN’s most advanced and high-quality free trade agreements. The agreement entered into force for Australia in January 2010.
AANZFTA Upgrade (Second Protocol, 2025)
The Second Protocol to Amend AANZFTA—known as the AANZFTA Upgrade—entered into force on 21 April 2025. It modernises the agreement by streamlining processes to help businesses better access existing tariff preferences and expands market access opportunities and strengthens regulatory certainty for service providers.
The AANZFTA Upgrade has been ratified and implemented by all AANZFTA Parties except Indonesia and the Philippines. Trade with Indonesia and the Philippines under AANZFTA remains subject to the First Protocol. Please refer to the AANZFTA Official Documents page.
AANZFTA is complemented by Australia’s bilateral free trade agreements with Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and New Zealand, providing additional pathways for trade and investment across the region.
Like Australia, some AANZFTA parties are also members of other major regional trade agreements including the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership (CPTPP) and the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership

Key outcomes and benefits
The upgraded AANZFTA reflects the evolving needs of businesses and consumers, strengthening Australian trade and investment ties in one of the most dynamic regions in the world. It introduces new chapters and provisions across key areas including:
- micro, small and medium sized enterprises
- trade and sustainable development
- education services
- e-commerce
- competition
- customs procedures and trade facilitation
- trade in goods
- rules of origin
- rade in services
- investment
The upgraded Agreement will support Australian service suppliers, goods exporters and investors to grow their reach into ASEAN with greater confidence and accelerate trade diversification.
